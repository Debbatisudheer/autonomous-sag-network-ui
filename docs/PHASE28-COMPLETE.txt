PHASE 28 — HIGHER-FIDELITY WIRELESS / NTN CHANNEL

Status: Implemented
Baseline: Phase 27 final-clean-complete

Added:
- Dedicated stateful deterministic NTN channel model package.
- 3GPP TR 38.811 elevation-dependent LOS probabilities.
- 3GPP TR 38.811 shadow-fading and NLOS clutter tables for S/Ka bands.
- Per-link correlated shadow fading.
- Doppler-derived coherence-time model.
- Correlated Rician LOS / Rayleigh NLOS fast fading.
- Explicit geometry-scaled gas/rain/cloud attenuation inputs.
- Scintillation and building-entry input terms.
- LinkBudget integration.
- Real SGP4 satellite-link integration.
- Unified-candidate channel application helper.
- Deterministic channel fingerprinting, tests, and Phase 28 demo.

Model boundary:
- Not a complete 3GPP NR PHY or full TR 38.811 link-level channel.
- Weather attenuation inputs are scenario/external-adapter values; full ITU-R site-climate algorithms remain a later fidelity extension.
- Full TDL/CDL, antenna-array spatial filtering, polarization/XPD, ray tracing, and receiver impairments remain outside this phase.

References:
- 3GPP TR 38.811 NTN channel model, especially Sections 6.6.1 and 6.6.2.
- ITU-R P.618-14 Earth-space propagation.
- ITU-R P.676-13 atmospheric gaseous attenuation.
